02 Company Overview
Who we are
Namutek Logistics Limited is a Zambian company providing transportation, logistics and construction
services to businesses and organisations. We specialise in the movement of goods and materials,
supporting clients with freight transportation, haulage and logistics solutions across Zambia and
regional routes.
What we do
We move goods, materials and equipment, coordinate their movement from origin to destination, and
support construction and infrastructure projects with the transportation and site services they require.
Our work covers both individual assignments and ongoing operational requirements.
How we work
Our work is centred on dependable services that help clients move goods, support their operations
and keep projects progressing. With a focus on professionalism, safety, reliability and efficient
service, Namutek is building a strong presence in Zambia's logistics, transportation and construction
sectors.
